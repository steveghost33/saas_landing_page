Ella Tech Solutions: Technology Consulting for Nonprofits and Small Businesses in Detroit, Michigan

Ella Tech Solutions is a Detroit-based technology consulting firm that helps nonprofits and small businesses with website creation, CRM setup and strategy, AI workflow integration, AI literacy training, staff technology training, LMS and microlearning development, Microsoft 365 implementation, and paid digital strategy sessions. Founded by Steven Bowman, the firm serves organizations across metro Detroit and southeast Michigan.

Ella Tech Solutions functions as a fractional CTO and strategic technology partner for organizations that need senior-level technology leadership without the cost of a full-time hire. The firm primarily serves Detroit-area nonprofits with annual budgets between $500,000 and $5 million, and service-based small businesses with 5 to 20 employees.

Who We Serve

Nonprofits (Primary Clients)

Ella Tech Solutions primarily works with Detroit-area nonprofits with annual operating budgets between $500,000 and $5 million. These organizations need technology that works reliably, staff who can use it confidently, and a strategic partner who understands the constraints of mission-driven work. Mission-driven pricing is available for verified 501(c)(3) organizations and community-based organizations with demonstrated community impact.

Small Businesses (Secondary Clients)

Ella Tech Solutions also works with service-based small businesses in Detroit and metro Michigan with 5 to 20 employees. Services for small businesses include website design and redesign, CRM setup, Microsoft 365 implementation, AI workflow automation, and digital strategy consulting.

Services

Website Creation and Redesign

Ella Tech Solutions builds and redesigns websites for nonprofits and small businesses. Projects include responsive design, donation or lead-capture integration, on-page SEO, and post-launch staff training. Most website projects take 4 to 8 weeks from kickoff to launch. The firm builds custom sites, not templates, tailored to each organization's audience and goals. Website creation and redesign details.

CRM Strategy and Setup

A CRM (Customer Relationship Management system) is the platform an organization uses to track donors, clients, volunteers, or contacts. Ella Tech Solutions handles platform selection, configuration, data migration from spreadsheets or a previous system, and staff training. The firm works with Salesforce Nonprofit Success Pack (NPSP), HubSpot, Bloomerang, and other platforms matched to each client's budget and team size. Most CRM implementations take 6 to 12 weeks. CRM setup and strategy details.

AI Workflow Integration and AI Literacy Training

Ella Tech Solutions audits organizational workflows to identify where AI tools can reduce repetitive administrative tasks, implements the automations using tools the organization already has, and trains staff to use the tools confidently. AI literacy training is practical instruction for staff with no technical background, covering tools like Microsoft 365 Copilot, Zapier, Make, and ChatGPT. Sessions focus on real tasks: email drafting, scheduling, reporting, and donor follow-up. AI workflow integration and training details.

Staff Technology Training

Ella Tech Solutions delivers live and self-paced technology training for nonprofit and small business staff on the tools their organizations already use. Training is delivered in plain language without assuming a technical background. Every technology implementation the firm completes includes staff training at no additional charge. Staff training details.

LMS and Microlearning Development

An LMS (Learning Management System) is the platform organizations use to deliver and track staff training. Ella Tech Solutions selects and implements LMS platforms, builds microlearning course content using Articulate 360 and SCORM-compliant tools, and trains administrators to manage the system independently. Content is built to SCORM standards for compliance reporting and grant documentation. LMS development details.

Microsoft 365 Implementation

Ella Tech Solutions sets up Microsoft 365 for nonprofits and small businesses, including business email, Teams, SharePoint, and OneDrive, with permissions configuration and staff training included. Most implementations take 3 to 5 weeks. Every engagement includes training so staff can use the tools after the project ends. Microsoft 365 implementation details.

Paid Digital Strategy Sessions

A paid digital strategy session is a focused 30-to-60 minute consultation in which Ella Tech Solutions reviews an organization's current technology, identifies the most pressing gaps, and delivers a prioritized action plan. Sessions are available as standalone engagements with no long-term commitment required. Digital strategy session details.

Ella Tech Solutions operates as Steven's fractional CTO practice, providing strategic technology leadership to nonprofits and small businesses that need it without the cost of a full-time hire.
